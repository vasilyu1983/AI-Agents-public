## Domain Knowledge

- 2026-07-11: DORA's core delivery metrics expanded from four to five in the 2025 "State of AI-Assisted Software Development" report — rework rate was added as the fifth (instability) metric, alongside deployment frequency, lead time, failed-deployment recovery time (throughput), and change failure rate (instability). Do not describe DORA as tracking "four metrics" in 2026+ guidance. A newer companion report, "ROI of AI-assisted Software Development" (~April 2026, dora.dev/ai/roi/report/), introduced the "instability tax" and J-curve ROI framing — it is a companion to the 2025 core report, not a replacement; cite both by their distinct titles. Backstage adoption reality (industry-reported, not an official CNCF/Backstage statistic) commonly plateaus around ~10% unless a team staffs 2-3 dedicated engineers for ~12 months and treats it as a product — this belongs in "when NOT to build an IDP" judgment, not just the tool-comparison table. OpenTofu (CNCF project since April 2025) has functionally diverged from Terraform's OSS binary (state encryption, early variable evaluation, OCI registry support, etc.) — treat "OpenTofu is just a drop-in Terraform fork" as stale.
