# ai-deep-research — Raw Learnings

Append-only. Consolidated periodically into `learnings.consolidated.md` via `agents-skills-feedback-loop/scripts/consolidate.py`.

- 2026-07-11: A July 2026 audit found OpenAI's Deep Research feature guide (`developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/deep-research`) still recommending `o3-deep-research`/`o4-mini-deep-research` as current on the same day the separate deprecations page (`developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations`) listed both as shut down 2026-07-23, replacement `gpt-5.5-pro`. Lesson generalized into a new Known Trap and Fact-Checking rule: for any native deep-research agent, check the provider's deprecations/sunset page independently — never take a feature guide's silence on deprecation as proof a model is still supported, since guide and deprecations pages update on different cadences.
- 2026-07-11: xAI shipped documented, API-invocable `web_search`/`x_search` server-side tools (`docs.x.ai/developers/tools/`) since the skill's last check — Grok DeepSearch is no longer "product-UI only." Still no one-call report-generating deep-research model comparable to the other four agents; it's a tool you compose into your own P2/P4 loop, not a fifth native deep-research product.
- 2026-07-11: Perplexity's `sonar-deep-research` pricing is genuinely ambiguous across secondary sources on whether a flat per-request fee stacks on top of token/search-query metering. When a fact can't be pinned to one primary source without contradiction, the right move is to widen the stated range and flag the ambiguity explicitly rather than pick one secondary source's number and present it with false precision.
