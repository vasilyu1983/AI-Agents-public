# Neural Evidence to Product Decisions

## Evidence record

For every neural recommendation record three separate rows: mechanism evidence; task-specific measurement validity; product-intervention evidence. Give the source, population, task, uncertainty and missing evidence for each. A mechanism may exist while the proposed digital intervention remains unsupported.

| Observation | Confounds / alternative explanations | Supported next step |
|---|---|---|
| GSR rises | Movement, heat, orienting or distress; valence unknown | Check signal quality and context; pair with task and self-report |
| High DAU, low satisfaction | Low value, friction, reminders, obligation or conflicting goals | Investigate behavioral value; do not diagnose dopamine wanting/liking |
| Frontal alpha difference | Reference, artifacts, baseline and asymmetry formula | State formula; conventionally alpha power is inverse to activation; avoid individual targeting |
| DMN or insula BOLD | Multiple cognitive functions; slow hemodynamics | Do not infer a unique process or transmitter from activation |
| Warmth copy helps a survey score | Care expectations, clarity or service quality | Report behavioral effect; no oxytocin release or half-life cadence inference |

Reverse inference from a nonspecific biomarker to a particular emotional/neural state requires task-specific discrimination evidence. Mechanism names, circuit labels and citations alone cannot supply it.

## Operational defaults

Choose reminder timing from user preference, timezone and quiet hours; randomize eligible timing alternatives. Consolidation research does not establish 7–9pm or a 30-minute post-wake mandate. No 90-second cortisol deadline, 200ms digital-trust deadline, 90% consistency requirement or seven-unlock neural threshold is supported here. A chosen cadence limit or service target must be labeled a product/safety default with rationale and review criteria.

Physiological research needs informed participation and data classification. Document an Article 6 basis for personal data; assess health inference or biometric unique identification and record an Article 9 condition if applicable. Explicit consent is not the only possible condition. Privacy classification and research consent are distinct.

## Minimal deliverable

Return the proposed behavioral outcome, three evidence rows (only if making a neural claim), confound controls, field validation plan, user-welfare/opt-out measures, and verdict supported / conditional / insufficient. Mark absent product-effect evidence; do not fill it with mechanism speculation.

## Regression checks

Reject inverted alpha interpretation; physiology-as-trust diagnosis; cortisol/oxytocin-derived UX timers; mandatory clock windows; blanket special-category/explicit-consent classification. Preserve quiet hours, accurate disclosures and user controls independently of neural claims.

## Primary and official sources

- [Acute stress EEG experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8850739/): greater right alpha interpreted as greater left cortical activation; state the scoring convention.
- [Endocrine time-kinetics study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8626864/): minute-scale cortisol latency, not a 90-second UX rule.
- [Human NREM replay recordings](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29234075/): evidence of replay, not fixed app-notification windows.
- [Registered oxytocin/trust replication](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-020-0878-x): no universal trust effect.
- [ICO data-protection concepts](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/biometric-data-guidance-biometric-recognition/key-data-protection-concepts/): identification and health-purpose classification.
- [ICO lawful processing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/biometric-data-guidance-biometric-recognition/how-do-we-process-biometric-data-lawfully/): assess the basis and applicable condition; current legal facts require live verification.
