# Primitive: Interoception & Somatic Markers

## Definition

Interoception concerns sensing and interpreting internal bodily signals. The somatic marker hypothesis proposes a role for bodily signals in decision-making. Insular activity is associated with multiple processes; product context, color, countdowns and cancellations do not identify insular activation, threat appraisal or a stored somatic marker in a particular user.

## Evidence boundary

Apply [the evidence-to-product contract](../../../references/evidence-to-product.md) before a mechanism claim. Bounce, cancellation, eye tracking and clicks do not diagnose neural circuitry. Distinguish observed behavior, candidate explanations and measured mechanisms. Neural claims require valid distinct measurements, selectivity and base-rate reasoning, plus intervention evidence for a design prescription. Do not invent timing rules or a neuroscience-derived uplift.

## When to use

Use when body-state awareness is part of the product or an explicit consented research question. For ordinary pressure, pacing or error-recovery questions, use behavioral and usability measures first.

## Inputs and outputs

Collect user-reported experience, task context, truthful stakes, pacing alternatives and consented measurement plan. Do not infer anxiety from referral source. Return optional pacing/content variants and measures of pressure, comprehension, satisfaction and task outcomes. Body-state invitations should be optional and audience-tested; they need not help every user.

## Failure modes

| Observation | Alternatives to investigate | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Product feels clinical | Language, content relevance, accessibility, unmet expectations | Test comprehension and tone; do not diagnose absent positive insular engagement |
| Cancellation after error | Functional failure, poor recovery, price, confusion | Investigate and improve recovery; no somatic-marker diagnosis |
| Reported anxiety increases | Pressure, confusing requirements, poor content fit | Examine reports and remove manufactured urgency; neural state remains unknown |

## Worked example

Illustrative onboarding includes a red progress bar, countdowns and a pulsing CTA, with 18% day-one cancellation. That rate supplies no circuit diagnosis or causal attribution. Ask users about pressure and comprehension, inspect performance and pricing, and compare clearer user-paced variants. Make motion optional and show truthful results with an immediately available next action; impose no unexplained five-second delay. Measure cancellation completion as well as retention.

## Ethical boundary

Avoid manufactured pressure and unsupported health promises. Obtain informed research consent, classify personal-data processing and consult the applicable privacy owner for lawful bases and special-category conditions when collecting or inferring health data.
