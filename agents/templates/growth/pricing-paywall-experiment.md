# Growth Template: Pricing And Paywall Experiment

Use when pricing, packaging, paywall timing, or upgrade logic may be blocking growth.

## Route First

- Use `expert-board` in `monetization` mode for pricing, packaging, plan structure, paywall timing, revenue quality, and cohort safety.
- Use `expert-board` in `growth` mode only when monetization is one possible bottleneck among product, positioning, and channel problems.

## Required Inputs

- Current pricing and packaging
- Upgrade or checkout funnel
- Activation and retention by cohort
- Revenue by plan or product
- Support/sales objections
- Refund, churn, or cancellation signals
- Competitor pricing only as secondary context

## Experiment Brief

```text
Goal: evaluate one monetization change without damaging trust or cohort comparability.

Candidate change:
- Pricing:
- Packaging:
- Paywall timing:
- Trial or free tier:
- Upgrade path:

Safety checks:
- Which cohort is protected?
- What is the rollback path?
- What is the minimum sample or observation window?
- What churn/refund/support signal stops the test?
- Which metric proves value, not just conversion?
```

## Output Contract

```text
Monetization recommendation:
1. Bottleneck: price, packaging, timing, value communication, or trust.
2. Smallest safe experiment.
3. Cohort and rollout boundary.
4. Primary metric and guardrail metrics.
5. Expected direction of impact.
6. Stop and rollback criteria.
7. Execution team.
```

## Guardrails

- Do not use competitor pricing as the main proof.
- Do not raise price without checking value communication and activation.
- Do not optimize trial starts if downstream retention or refunds worsen.
