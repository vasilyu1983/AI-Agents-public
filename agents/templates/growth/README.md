# Growth Templates

Operational templates for the `expert-board` growth, monetization, and growth-experiments boards, and adjacent product reviews. Use these when the workflow or team is selected and the missing piece is a tighter launch brief, scorecard, or output shape.

## Templates

| File | Use when |
|---|---|
| [market-entry-wedge.md](market-entry-wedge.md) | A product exists, but the first serious audience and channel are unclear |
| [channel-experiment-scorecard.md](channel-experiment-scorecard.md) | Several channels look plausible and the team needs one primary test |
| [activation-retention-diagnosis.md](activation-retention-diagnosis.md) | Acquisition is not the only suspected problem; activation or retention may be broken |
| [pricing-paywall-experiment.md](pricing-paywall-experiment.md) | Monetization change is possible but churn, trust, or cohort damage is the risk |

## Rules

- Pick one template per run.
- Require first-party metrics when available; public market guesses are secondary.
- Force a single primary recommendation and one backup path.
- Include kill criteria before execution starts.
- Route execution to the smallest team that can ship the next test.
