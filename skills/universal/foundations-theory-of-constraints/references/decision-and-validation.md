# Constraint intervention and allocation worksheet

## Intake and deliverable

State system goal, accepted throughput/quality threshold, representative task mix, measurement window, resource capacities and uncertainty, demand caps, dependencies, mandatory obligations, undesirable effects and candidate policies. Deliver candidate-vs-verified constraint evidence, exploit/subordinate/elevate options, throughput and quality guardrails, allocation assumptions, calibrated buffers and follow-up measurement.

## Verify the constraint

Queues, utilization and latency share screen candidates; they do not prove a throughput limiter. Change one candidate's effective capacity or policy through a controlled intervention, replay or natural experiment at fixed mix/quality. Record changes in accepted end-to-end throughput, competing explanations and confidence. A deep queue with no throughput response stays a candidate. Re-evaluate after each intervention and handle near-tied/shared constraints jointly.

CRT/FRT logic and policy-removal thought experiments generate hypotheses, not empirical confirmation. Required legal/safety/quality work is a feasibility constraint, not automatically a stale policy to abolish. Preserve it during subordination.

## Allocate work honestly

T/CU ranks divisible, comparable marginal throughput under one verified constraint and justified demand/horizon assumptions. Capacity10 with indivisible initiatives (CU,T)=(6,12),(5,9),(5,9) makes ratio-greedy choose12 while the second and third jointly yield18. Dependencies, multiple capacities, uncertainty or indivisibility require explicit constrained optimization; hand off to foundations-mathematical-optimization with decisions, objective, resources and obligations. No LP/MILP solver is introduced here.

Include actual incremental per-use API/cloud and transaction charges in TVC. Exclude allocated fixed/step-fixed expense but disclose elevation and horizon changes. Feature revenue estimates are uncertain hypotheses; show ranges rather than fabricated ROI.

## Buffers and follow-up

Estimate task variation, correlations and contention; select buffer against delivery confidence and consequences. Fifty-percent and traffic-light zones are examples, not constants. Show completed-work fraction and consumed-buffer fraction separately and state the local ratio/zones. Review quality, risk and delivery evidence alongside buffer burn. Different variability can justify different buffers even at equal medians.

Source boundary: canonical Goldratt/Dettmer texts in data/sources.json support the TOC process; empirical causal attribution and mathematical optimization require their own methods. The greedy counterexample is a checkable arithmetic construction, not a claimed study result.
