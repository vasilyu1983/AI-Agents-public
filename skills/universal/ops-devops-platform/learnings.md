## Domain Knowledge

- [2026-07-11] DORA's core delivery metrics expanded from four to five in the 2025 "State of AI-Assisted Software Development" report — rework rate was added as the fifth (instability) metric, alongside deployment frequency, lead time, failed-deployment recovery time (throughput), and change failure rate (instability). Do not describe DORA as tracking "four metrics" in 2026+ guidance. A companion report, "ROI of AI-assisted Software Development" (dora.dev/ai/roi/report/), introduced the "instability tax" and J-curve ROI framing — it is a companion to the 2025 core report, not a replacement; cite both by their distinct titles.
