# Delivery Best Practices (Hand-off to Execution)

- **Planning & scope**
 - Define acceptance criteria, non-goals, and constraints
 - Align dependencies and owners; record open risks and mitigations
- **Backlog quality**
 - Slice work by user outcome; avoid large “epics” without definition
 - Definition of Ready/Done visible to the team ([checklists](../assets/ops/template-dor-dod.md))
- **Engineering handoff**
 - PRD/spec links, API/contract notes, data flows, success metrics
 - Edge cases, error states, and rollout/rollback plan documented
- **Execution cadence**
 - Daily surfacing of blockers; visible burn-down or flow metrics
 - WIP limits per team; explicit policy for unplanned work
 - Ceremony formats and async alternatives: [agile-ceremony-patterns.md](agile-ceremony-patterns.md), [remote-async-workflows.md](remote-async-workflows.md)
 - Debt allocation and tracking: [technical-debt-management.md](technical-debt-management.md)
- **Quality gates**
 - Test strategy per change, feature flags, canary/gradual rollout
 - Monitoring/alerting added with new functionality
- **Review & learning**
 - After-action reviews for incidents/launches; track follow-ups to closure
